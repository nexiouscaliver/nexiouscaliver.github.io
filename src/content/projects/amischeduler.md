Built in my third year at Amity University Mumbai as a coursework project,
then adopted by the university. Faculty log in with their university email,
book any of 15 venue halls, and approvals flow through an automated
chairperson workflow instead of paper. It served 35+ faculty daily, processed
200+ bookings a month with zero downtime, and the Vice Chancellor recognized
it for its contribution to university infrastructure.

It lived on the university intranet, which is why there is no public link.
